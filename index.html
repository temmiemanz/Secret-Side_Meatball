// lore
In 1985, a year into the world falling apart, Johnny Wellman found someone else trying to hold it together. They called themselves a hero team — just two of them. Johnny went by Meatball, his arms at the time reshaping into something that looked exactly like what the name implied, and he could heal others with them. His partner went by The Crimson Fist — or just Crim. He'd made the coat himself: dark black jumpsuit, crimson with black lining. They were best friends. They made it work.
A year in, someone tore Crim in half on top of him. Johnny suffocated the man with his morphing arms before the rage had time to become a thought. He buried Crim. He kept the coat. He wears it on his shoulders now, every day, because some things you don't take off.
By the 2000s he's working with the Archivist Network — cataloguing, researching, moving through the world quietly in a suit he loves more than almost anything. In conversation with coworkers one day he mentions he's been tracing the source of the awakening. They turn on him instantly. He doesn't know why. He's still trying to figure it out, which means he's a wanted man chasing an answer he isn't even sure exists yet.
He can look like anyone at any time. He keeps the black hair long, the beard a few days past stubble, the suit always on, the crimson coat always over his shoulders. He smokes. Crimson, purple, and black — that's his world. It has been since 1986.
